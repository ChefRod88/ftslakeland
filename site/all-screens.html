1cOur History — the unbroken line, vertical
Florida Theological Seminary · Our History Apply

1901 — 2026 · One hundred twenty-five years

Twice the buildings burned.
The classes never stopped.

Fire took the dormitory in 1921 and the academic building a few years later. For eleven years the seminary met in the sanctuaries of Harmony Baptist and St. Paul Baptist, because the work was never the building.

1901

Organized in October at Providence Baptist Church in Bartow, under the South Florida Association, as Florida Baptist Seminary. Nine students, three teachers, no building.

1902

Moved to Lakeland for a more central location, meeting in local churches while a campus was raised.

1903–04

The dormitory and academic buildings are erected. The seminary has a home of its own.

archival photo — original campus, c. 1904
1921

The dormitory burns down in November. Classes move into the sanctuaries of Harmony Baptist and St. Paul Baptist within the week.

1925

The State of Florida issues a charter of incorporation under the name Florida Seminary.

1927–28

The academic building burns. Teaching continues uninterrupted in borrowed pews.

1932

A new building rises and the seminary returns to its own property after eleven years in borrowed sanctuaries.

Today

Campuses in Lakeland, Dunedin, Lake City, and Jacksonville. Alumni pastor churches across the South.

2026

Accreditation granted. One hundred and twenty-five years of teaching, formally recognized.

We are now an accredited institution.

After a full review of our curriculum, faculty credentials, and student outcomes, Florida Theological Seminary and Bible College has been granted accreditation by [accrediting body]. It does not change our commitment to rightly dividing the Word. It changes who else has to recognize it.

Recognition

Your degree travels

Credentialing boards, denominational bodies, and chaplaincy programs recognize coursework from accredited institutions.

Continuation

A path to further study

Graduates are positioned to continue toward graduate work elsewhere, without repeating what they have mastered.

Assurance

A reviewed standard

Curriculum and outcomes are measured against a published standard on a fixed cycle. The rigor is documented, not asserted.

What it means if you are already enrolled

Nothing you have completed is lost. Coursework taken before 2026 is being recorded against the accredited transcript, and the registrar is contacting current students by campus through September.
