Programs, requirements, and tuition in one document. Download it or ask us to mail a copy.

The form above, plus a letter from your pastor. No transcripts needed for foundational study.

Sis. Linda Silas confirms your courses and campus by phone, then emails the registration form.

Evenings and weekends at the campus you chose. Books are available through the seminary at cost.
