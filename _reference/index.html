Accredited 2026

What accreditation
changes for you.

Accreditation by [accrediting body] is an outside review of how we teach, who we hire, and what a degree from this seminary is worth. It does not change our commitment to rightly dividing the Word of God. It changes who else has to recognize it.

Recognition

Your degree travels

Credentialing boards, denominational bodies, and chaplaincy programs recognize coursework from accredited institutions. Your transcript now carries that weight outside our walls.

Continuation

A path to further study

Students who complete a program here are positioned to continue toward graduate work elsewhere, without repeating what they have already mastered.

Assurance

A reviewed standard

Curriculum, faculty credentials, and student outcomes are now measured against a published standard and reviewed on a fixed cycle. The rigor is documented, not asserted.

Since 1901

Twice the buildings burned.
The classes never stopped.

Fire took the dormitory in 1921 and the academic building a few years later. For eleven years the seminary met in the sanctuaries of Harmony Baptist and St. Paul Baptist, because the work was never the building.

1901

Organized in October at Providence Baptist Church in Bartow, under the South Florida Association, as Florida Baptist Seminary.

1902

Moved to Lakeland for a more central location, meeting in local churches while a campus was raised.

1903–04

The dormitory and academic buildings are erected. The seminary has a home of its own.

1921

The dormitory burns down in November. Classes move into the sanctuaries of Harmony Baptist and St. Paul Baptist.

1925

The State of Florida issues a charter of incorporation under the name Florida Seminary.

1927–28

The academic building burns. Teaching continues uninterrupted in borrowed pews.

1932

A new building rises and the seminary returns to its own property after eleven years.

Today

Campuses in Lakeland, Dunedin, Lake City, and Jacksonville. Alumni pastor churches across the South.

2026

Accreditation granted. One hundred and twenty-five years of teaching, formally recognized.
